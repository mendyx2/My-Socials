# Visual brand (taken from my current banner)

Used for carousels, banners and highlight covers.

## Colors (sampled from the banner; adjust to exact values if I have them)
| Role | Hex (approx.) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#262A35` (dark charcoal-navy) | Slide backgrounds |
| Accent | `#9DBAF4` (soft periwinkle blue) | Headings, highlights, glow |
| Text | `#FFFFFF` / soft grey | Body text |
| Card | translucent white over the background ("glass" cards) | Stat and step cards |

## Type
- **Display:** elegant high-contrast serif (like the name on the banner). Free look-alikes: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display (Google Fonts).
- **Body / labels:** clean geometric sans, wide letter-spacing for small caps labels. Free options: Montserrat, Poppins, Inter.
- **Accent:** handwritten script for the tagline only ("Code. Automate. Elevate."). Free options: Caveat, Dancing Script.

## Elements
- "Oi" ring logo + "OI.Dev" wordmark
- Line icons (globe, phone, code window, brain) → free icon sets: Lucide or Tabler
- Soft blue glow and rounded glass cards
- Portfolio link pill at the bottom of CTA slides
