## Banner review (honest)

The design is polished and on-brand. The problem is **fit**, not looks:

1. **Wrong shape for LinkedIn.** It's 1600×639 (≈2.5:1); LinkedIn banners are 1584×396 (4:1). LinkedIn will crop roughly the top and bottom ~19% each, which removes the **OI.Dev logo and the portfolio URL**.
2. **The profile photo covers the bottom-left** on desktop, right where the tagline and "Website Development" icon sit. On mobile the photo sits in a different spot and the right-hand cards shrink to unreadable.
3. **Your photo appears twice** (banner and profile photo). The banner space is more valuable for a CTA or proof.
4. **The services are listed twice** (icon row on the left and the cards on the right). Pick one.
5. **No "book a call" message**, which is now the main goal.

Suggested LinkedIn banner (1584×396; the tool can render this from `brand.md`):
- Left ~25% empty (profile photo area) → middle: **name + "Website · Flutter · AI Automation Developer"** → right: **"Book a free call →"** and the booking/portfolio link.
- Keep the dark background, periwinkle accent, serif name and "Code. Automate. Elevate." script.
- Drop the photo and the duplicate service row. Keep the 3 service icons small, in one line.
- Keep critical text away from the edges and the bottom-left.

The current full design still works well as a **portfolio website hero**, a **Facebook/X cover** or a **pinned Instagram post**.
