# Content pillars (draft, edit freely)

Every idea the tool generates is tagged with one pillar. Aim for a mix each week, weighted toward what gets calls (pillars 3 and 4).

1. **Web & frontend:** performance, SEO, modern UI, "what makes a business website convert", teardown/redesign examples.
2. **Flutter & mobile:** cross-platform trade-offs explained for business owners, build-in-public, app launch lessons.
3. **AI automation for businesses:** what to automate first, before/after workflows, tools explained simply, AI news translated into "what this means for your business".
4. **Proof & process:** client results, case studies, how I work, behind the scenes. This is the pillar that most directly leads to booked calls.
5. **Personal (light, ~10%):** my journey as a developer, lessons, wins. Builds trust and makes the brand human.

## News sources to watch (starter list, to refine)
- Web: web.dev, Smashing Magazine, CSS-Tricks, Vercel/Next.js blog, Chrome developers blog
- Flutter: Flutter blog (Medium), Dart blog, Flutter GitHub releases
- AI/automation: OpenAI, Anthropic and Google AI blogs; n8n / Make / Zapier blogs; Hacker News; Product Hunt
- Business angle: Google News RSS for "small business AI", "automation", "mobile app trends"
